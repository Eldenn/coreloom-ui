# UML Diagram

Software diagrams drawn from one typed model: **class**, **state**, **entity–relationship** and **C4** (context, container, component) diagrams on a canvas laid out automatically, and **sequence** diagrams as an SVG. The model is plain JSON (`UmlSpec` in `@/lib/uml`); `parseMermaid` reads the matching Mermaid text into it and `toMermaid` writes it back, with no `mermaid` dependency and nothing rendered as HTML.

**The consumer provides** `diagram` (a `UmlSpec`: Mermaid read with `parseMermaid(text).diagram`, JSON from outside checked field by field with `parseUmlJson(text).diagram`, or one built in code). Optionally: `editable` (false; elements can be dragged, and "Arrange" lays them out again), `onLayoutChange(layout)` (the positions after a drag, to store in the diagram's `layout`), `height` (480; the sequence view scrolls past it), `controls` (true: zoom in, zoom out, fit, and arrange when editable), `label` (the accessible name; default: the kind of diagram), `selection` / `onSelectionChange` (element ids and relation indexes; uncontrolled by default), `onConnect(from, to)` (with `editable`: each element shows a dot to drag onto another; add the relation with `addRelation`), `onDelete(selection)` (Delete or Backspace on the canvas; remove it with `deleteItems`), `onRename(id, name)` (with `editable`: F2 or a double-click on an element opens a name field over it; apply it with `renameElement`), `onElementDrop(type, position)` (with `editable`: something dragged onto the canvas with the type in `UML_DRAG_TYPE` data (from `@/lib/uml-edit`), at its position in the diagram's coordinates) and `ref` (a `UmlDiagramHandle`: `toImage('svg' | 'png')` resolves to a data URL of the whole drawing, on the background it sits on, without the selection or the connection dots).

- Layout: layers along `direction` (TB by default; LR, BT, RL), parents and wholes first; namespaces, composite states and C4 boundaries are frames that grow around what they hold. Positions in `layout` win over the automatic ones.
- Notation: inheritance and realization end in a hollow triangle, composition in a filled diamond, aggregation in a hollow one, dependencies are dashed; ER relations use crow's feet; C4 relations are dashed arrows with their technology in brackets. External C4 elements are grey and say "External" in their label line, never by colour alone.
- **Select area** (with `editable`, in the controls) turns dragging on the empty canvas into a box selection instead of panning; Shift-drag does the same at any time. With two or more elements selected in the same frame, a bar at the bottom lines them up (left, centre, right, top, middle, bottom) or spaces them evenly (three or more); the new positions go to `onLayoutChange`. `alignBoxes` in `@/lib/uml-arrange` does the same for your own tools.
- Keyboard: Tab moves through elements and then relations; each says what it is ("Class: Order, 2 attributes, 2 methods"; "Duck inherits from Animal"). When editable, a selected element moves with the arrow keys. Selected elements have a ring and selected lines turn the primary colour. Dragging to connect has a keyboard path in your own UI (the Diagram Editor's Connect form).
- The sequence view is a focusable scrolling region; its steps are also an ordered list for screen readers (blocks as nested lists). With `selection` / `onSelectionChange`, a click on a participant, message, note or block selects it (Ctrl, ⌘ or Shift adds to the selection); steps are named by their place (`step:2`, `step:2.1.0` for the first step of a block's second section, see `stepId` in `@/lib/uml-edit-sequence`). Clicks are a shortcut: give people a keyboard path in your own UI (the Diagram Editor's step list).
- `parseMermaid` reads `classDiagram`, `sequenceDiagram`, `stateDiagram-v2`, `erDiagram`, `C4Context`, `C4Container` and `C4Component`. Lines it can't read come back as problems with their line number, and the rest still draws; styling lines (`style`, `classDef`…) are set aside with a warning.
- Not for flowcharts or free-form drawings: use the Node Editor for graphs people build by hand.

**In an app:** `pnpm dlx shadcn@latest add @coreloom/uml-diagram`.
