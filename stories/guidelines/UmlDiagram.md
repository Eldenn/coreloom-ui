# UML Diagram

Software diagrams drawn from one typed model: **class**, **state**, **entity–relationship**, **C4** (context, container, component), **use case**, **activity** and **deployment** diagrams on a canvas laid out automatically, and **sequence** diagrams as an SVG. The model is plain JSON (`UmlSpec` in `@/lib/uml`); `parseMermaid` and `parsePlantUml` read the matching Mermaid or PlantUML text into it and `toMermaid` / `toPlantUml` write it back, with no `mermaid` or PlantUML dependency and nothing rendered as HTML.

**The consumer provides** `diagram` (a `UmlSpec`: Mermaid read with `parseMermaid(text).diagram`, PlantUML with `parsePlantUml(text).diagram`, JSON from outside checked field by field with `parseUmlJson(text).diagram`, or one built in code). Optionally: `editable` (false; elements can be dragged, and "Arrange" lays them out again), `onLayoutChange(layout)` (the positions after a drag, to store in the diagram's `layout`), `height` (480; the sequence view scrolls past it), `controls` (true: zoom in, zoom out, fit, and arrange when editable), `label` (the accessible name; default: the kind of diagram), `selection` / `onSelectionChange` (element ids and relation indexes; uncontrolled by default), `onConnect(from, to)` (with `editable`: each element shows a dot to drag onto another; add the relation with `addRelation`), `onDelete(selection)` (Delete or Backspace on the canvas; remove it with `deleteItems`), `onRename(id, name)` (with `editable`: F2 or a double-click on an element opens a name field over it; apply it with `renameElement`), `onElementDrop(type, position)` (with `editable`: something dragged onto the canvas with the type in `UML_DRAG_TYPE` data (from `@/lib/uml-edit`), at its position in the diagram's coordinates) and `ref` (a `UmlDiagramHandle`: `toImage('svg' | 'png')` resolves to a data URL of the whole drawing, on the background it sits on, without the selection or the connection dots).

- Layout: layers along `direction` (TB by default, LR for use cases; LR, BT, RL), parents and wholes first; namespaces, composite states, C4 boundaries, use case systems, activity partitions and deployment nodes that hold others are frames that grow around what they hold. Positions in `layout` win over the automatic ones.
- Notation: inheritance and realization end in a hollow triangle, composition in a filled diamond, aggregation in a hollow one, dependencies are dashed; ER relations use crow's feet; C4 relations are dashed arrows with their technology in brackets. Use cases are ellipses and actors stick figures; «include» and «extend» are dashed arrows with their keyword. Activities have a filled start, a ringed end, a crossed flow end, diamonds for decisions and merges, bars for forks and joins, and guards in brackets (`[paid]`). Deployment nodes, devices and execution environments are 3D boxes with their keyword («device»); artifacts, components, databases and clouds show an icon beside their name. External C4 elements are grey and say "External" in their label line, never by colour alone.
- **Select area** (with `editable`, in the controls) turns dragging on the empty canvas into a box selection instead of panning; Shift-drag does the same at any time. With two or more elements selected in the same frame, a bar at the bottom lines them up (left, centre, right, top, middle, bottom) or spaces them evenly (three or more); the new positions go to `onLayoutChange`. `alignBoxes` in `@/lib/uml-arrange` does the same for your own tools.
- Keyboard: Tab moves through elements and then relations; each says what it is ("Class: Order, 2 attributes, 2 methods"; "Duck inherits from Animal"). When editable, a selected element moves with the arrow keys. Selected elements have a ring and selected lines turn the primary colour. Dragging to connect has a keyboard path in your own UI (the Diagram Editor's Connect form).
- The sequence view is a focusable scrolling region; its steps are also an ordered list for screen readers (blocks as nested lists). With `selection` / `onSelectionChange`, a click on a participant, message, note or block selects it (Ctrl, ⌘ or Shift adds to the selection); steps are named by their place (`step:2`, `step:2.1.0` for the first step of a block's second section, see `stepId` in `@/lib/uml-edit-sequence`). Clicks are a shortcut: give people a keyboard path in your own UI (the Diagram Editor's step list).
- `parseMermaid` reads `classDiagram`, `sequenceDiagram`, `stateDiagram-v2`, `erDiagram`, `C4Context`, `C4Container`, `C4Component`, and `flowchart` / `graph` as an activity diagram (`((start))`, `(((end)))`, `{decision}`, `@{ shape: fork }`, subgraphs as partitions). Lines it can't read come back as problems with their line number, and the rest still draws; styling lines (`style`, `classDef`…) are set aside with a warning.
- `parsePlantUml` reads `@startuml` text and works out the kind from what it says: classes, states, sequences, entities, C4-PlantUML macros, use cases (`actor`, `usecase`, `(Name)`, `rectangle` around them, `<<include>>` / `<<extend>>`), deployment (`node`, `artifact`, `database`, `cloud`… nested in braces) and activities in the new syntax (`start`, `:action;`, `if … then … else … endif`, `while`, `repeat`, `fork`, `partition`, `|swimlanes|`). Skin parameters and styling come back as warnings.
- Writing is lossy in two places: `toMermaid` writes use case and deployment diagrams as a plain flowchart (Mermaid has no such diagrams), and `toPlantUml` writes activities in PlantUML's older arrow syntax, which `parsePlantUml` doesn't read back. Keep such diagrams as PlantUML, Mermaid or JSON respectively.
- Not for free-form drawings: use the Node Editor for graphs people build by hand.

**In an app:** `pnpm dlx shadcn@latest add @coreloom/uml-diagram`.
