# UML Diagram

> **Experimental.** This item may still change in a minor release, with an **Upgrading** note in the changelog. See [VERSIONING.md](https://github.com/Eldenn/coreloom-ui/blob/main/VERSIONING.md).

Software diagrams drawn from one typed model: **class**, **state**, **entity–relationship**, **C4** (context, container, component), **use case**, **activity**, **deployment**, **component** and **package** diagrams on a canvas laid out automatically, and **sequence** diagrams as an SVG. The model is plain JSON (`UmlSpec` in `@/lib/uml`); `parseMermaid` and `parsePlantUml` read the matching Mermaid or PlantUML text into it and `toMermaid` / `toPlantUml` write it back, with no `mermaid` or PlantUML dependency and nothing rendered as HTML.

**The consumer provides** `diagram` (a `UmlSpec`: Mermaid read with `parseMermaid(text).diagram`, PlantUML with `parsePlantUml(text).diagram`, JSON from outside checked field by field with `parseUmlJson(text).diagram`, or one built in code). Optionally: `editable` (false; elements can be dragged, and "Arrange" lays them out again), `onLayoutChange(layout)` (the positions after a drag, to store in the diagram's `layout`), `height` (480; the sequence view scrolls past it), `controls` (true: zoom in, zoom out, fit, full screen, and arrange when editable), `preview` (false: a still thumbnail, see below), `label` (the accessible name; default: the kind of diagram), `selection` / `onSelectionChange` (element ids and relation indexes; uncontrolled by default), `onConnect(from, to)` (with `editable`: each element shows a dot to drag onto another; add the relation with `addRelation`), `onDelete(selection)` (Delete or Backspace on the canvas; remove it with `deleteItems`), `onRename(id, name)` (with `editable`: F2 or a double-click on an element opens a name field over it; apply it with `renameElement`), `onElementDrop(type, position)` (with `editable`: something dragged onto the canvas with the type in `UML_DRAG_TYPE` data (from `@/lib/uml-edit`), at its position in the diagram's coordinates), `annotations` and `onAnnotationSelect(element)` (see below), `contextActions(target)`, `onDuplicate(selection)` and `onMemberDelete(element, index)` (a context menu, see below) and `ref` (a `UmlDiagramHandle`: `toImage('svg' | 'png', { light? })` resolves to a data URL of the whole drawing, on the background it sits on, without the selection or the connection dots; `light` draws it in the light theme whatever the page's, for paper. The SVG holds the drawing's HTML as the browser drew it: browsers show it, drawing tools don't, so give those the PNG. `printImage(url, title)` from `@/lib/uml-image` opens the browser's print dialog on that image alone, on a page of its size, where the reader saves it as a PDF).

- Layout: layers along `direction` (TB by default, LR for use cases; LR, BT, RL), parents and wholes first; namespaces, composite states, C4 boundaries, use case systems, activity partitions, deployment nodes, components and packages that hold others are frames that grow around what they hold. A component's ports sit on its edge, half on the left side and half on the right, and its frame grows tall enough for them. Positions in `layout` win over the automatic ones. `toMermaid` and `toPlantUml` keep `layout` in one comment line the renderers skip (`%% coreloom-layout {…}`, `' coreloom-layout {…}`), and `parseMermaid` / `parsePlantUml` read it back, keeping only positions of elements the text has.
- Notation: inheritance and realization end in a hollow triangle, composition in a filled diamond, aggregation in a hollow one, dependencies are dashed; ER relations use crow's feet; C4 relations are dashed arrows with their technology in brackets. Use cases are ellipses and actors stick figures; «include» and «extend» are dashed arrows with their keyword. Activities have a filled start, a ringed end, a crossed flow end, diamonds for decisions and merges, bars for forks and joins, and guards in brackets (`[paid]`). Deployment nodes, devices and execution environments are 3D boxes with their keyword («device»); artifacts, components, databases and clouds show an icon beside their name. External C4 elements are grey and say "External" in their label line, never by colour alone.
- **Select area** (with `editable`, in the controls) turns dragging on the empty canvas into a box selection instead of panning; Shift-drag does the same at any time. With two or more elements selected in the same frame, a bar at the bottom lines them up (left, centre, right, top, middle, bottom) or spaces them evenly (three or more); the new positions go to `onLayoutChange`. `alignBoxes` in `@/lib/uml-arrange` does the same for your own tools.
- **Resizing**: when the box changes size the drawing is fitted again, until the reader pans or zooms; their view is kept from then on.
- **Full screen**: a button in the controls shows the diagram on the whole screen (the Fullscreen API, on the diagram's box) and fits it again; Escape or the same button leaves, puts back the view from before, and focus goes back to the button. Sequence diagrams have the button in their top-right corner. Browsers that don't allow full screen don't show it.
- **Preview** (`preview`, for gallery cards and thumbnails): the drawing is fitted to its box (and again when the box changes size), with no controls, title, selection, dragging, panning or zoom; nothing inside takes focus, and the whole box is one image named by `label` (give it the document's or view's name). Clicks go through to what's under it, such as a card's link. Sequence diagrams are scaled down to fit, without their step list.
- Keyboard: Tab moves through elements and then relations; each says what it is ("Class: Order, 2 attributes, 2 methods"; "Duck inherits from Animal"). When editable, a selected element moves with the arrow keys. Selected elements have a ring and selected lines turn the primary colour. Dragging to connect has a keyboard path in your own UI (the Diagram Editor's Connect form).
- The sequence view is a focusable scrolling region; its steps are also an ordered list for screen readers (blocks as nested lists). With `selection` / `onSelectionChange`, a click on a participant, message, note or block selects it (Ctrl, ⌘ or Shift adds to the selection); steps are named by their place (`step:2`, `step:2.1.0` for the first step of a block's second section, see `stepId` in `@/lib/uml-edit-sequence`). Clicks are a shortcut: give people a keyboard path in your own UI (the Diagram Editor's step list).
- `parseMermaid` reads `classDiagram`, `sequenceDiagram`, `stateDiagram-v2`, `erDiagram`, `C4Context`, `C4Container`, `C4Component`, and `flowchart` / `graph` as an activity diagram (`((start))`, `(((end)))`, `{decision}`, `@{ shape: fork }`, subgraphs as partitions). Lines it can't read come back as problems with their line number, and the rest still draws; styling lines (`style`, `classDef`…) are set aside with a warning.
- `parsePlantUml` reads `@startuml` text and works out the kind from what it says: classes, states, sequences, entities, C4-PlantUML macros, use cases (`actor`, `usecase`, `(Name)`, `rectangle` around them, `<<include>>` / `<<extend>>`), deployment (`node`, `artifact`, `database`, `cloud`… nested in braces), components (`component`, `[Name]`, `interface`, `() Name`, `port` / `portin` / `portout` in braces; a plain line to an interface provides it, `-(` requires it, `-(0)-` is an assembly, `<<delegate>>` a delegation, a dotted arrow a dependency), packages (`package` in braces around `class`, `interface`, `enum` and components; `<<import>>` and `<<merge>>`; a text with packages and no class members or inheritance is a package diagram) and activities in the new syntax (`start`, `:action;`, `if … then … else … endif`, `while`, `repeat`, `fork`, `partition`, `|swimlanes|`). Skin parameters and styling come back as warnings.
- Writing is lossy in two places: `toMermaid` writes use case, deployment, component and package diagrams as a plain flowchart (Mermaid has no such diagrams), and `toPlantUml` writes activities in PlantUML's older arrow syntax, which `parsePlantUml` doesn't read back. Keep such diagrams as PlantUML, Mermaid or JSON respectively.
- **Annotations** (`annotations`, for the findings of a review against the code, a lint or a test run): each `{ element, status, count?, label? }` puts a ring in the status colour around the element and a badge on its corner, with the status's icon and the count from 2; badges keep their size (24px high) at every zoom. `status` is `ok`, `warning`, `error` or `unknown`; `label` names what was found ("Missing from the code", default: the status's name). `element` is an element id, or in a sequence diagram a participant id or a message's step id (`step:1`); ids the diagram doesn't have are skipped. Each element's accessible name ends with its finding, so the status is never in colour alone. With `onAnnotationSelect`, each badge is a button ("Findings for Order: Missing from the code") that hands the element's id back: show its findings, in a FindingCard say. Badges are marks only in a `preview`, and images made with `toImage` leave the annotations out.
- **Highlighted** (`highlighted`, a list of element ids): points elements out without selecting them, such as the active states while the app simulates a state machine or the element a list beside the drawing is about. Each gets an outline outside the selection and annotation rings, so an element can be selected, annotated and pointed out at once, and its accessible name ends with “highlighted”. Ids the diagram doesn't have are skipped. It changes nothing else: no focus move, no scroll, no selection. Sequence diagrams don't draw it, and images made with `toImage` leave it out.
- **Lines** go round the boxes, never behind them: each takes straight runs and right angles past every box but its two ends and the frames they sit in, lines that share a corridor or a side of a box run side by side, and a label is put where it covers no box and no other label. They are routed again whenever a box moves (`routeEdges` in `@/lib/uml-route`, a pure function).
- **Concurrent regions** of a composite state (`region` on a state, `--` between regions in Mermaid and PlantUML, `||` too in PlantUML): each region is laid out on its own with its own start, side by side (stacked when the diagram runs left to right), and a dashed line parts two regions. After someone drags states around, a line is drawn only where two regions no longer overlap.
- **Context menu** (`contextActions`): a right-click, a long press, or the context-menu key or Shift+F10 on a focused element or relation (on a focused sequence diagram: its selected participant or step) opens a menu on what's under it. With `editable`, the drawing's own entries come first, each only when its callback is given: Rename (`onRename`: the name field over the element), Duplicate (`onDuplicate`, with the element alone) and Delete (`onDelete`, with the element, relation or step alone) on an element; Delete on a relation or a step; Delete on a member (`onMemberDelete(element, index)`, `index` counting the attributes then the methods). Then a separator and what `contextActions(target)` returns: `{ label, icon?, destructive?, disabled?, onSelect }[]`. A drawing to read shows the app's entries only. With nothing to show, or without `contextActions`, no menu opens and the browser's own stays; a `preview` has none. A right-click on something not selected selects it alone, and the menu acts on that one target. On one of several selected elements, relations or steps (a member counts as its element), the selection stays and the menu is about all of them: Duplicate and Delete take the whole selection, Rename is left out, and the target is `{ type: 'selection', selection, targets, label }`, where `targets` lists each selected part as below (elements, then relations) and `label` counts them (`3 selected`). Otherwise the target (`UmlContextTarget`) is `{ type: 'element', id, label }`, `{ type: 'member', element, index, label }`, `{ type: 'relation', index, label }` (its place in the diagram's relations or transitions) or `{ type: 'step', id, label }` (a sequence step's id; a participant is an `element`). `label` is one line made from the diagram, the same for everyone: an element's name (`Payment`; a note's text; a nameless start or end its kind), a member after its element as the box shows it (`Payment.total: number`, `Payment.pay(amount)`, `Order.total: decimal` for an ER attribute), a relation's ends as the model has them and its label (`Order → Payment: pays`), a step the way someone would write it (`Client -> API: login()`, `-->` for a reply or a dotted line, `Note over API, DB: cached`, `activate API`, `loop every minute`). `SequenceDiagramView` takes `contextActions` too, for the app's entries.
- Not for free-form drawings: use the Node Editor for graphs people build by hand.

**In an app:** `pnpm dlx shadcn@latest add @coreloom/uml-diagram`.
