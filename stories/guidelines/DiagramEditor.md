# Diagram Editor

Software diagrams as text: Mermaid, PlantUML or Coreloom's UML JSON on the left, the drawing on the right with its own tools. Problems are underlined on their line and listed under the text, and what can be read still draws. The drawing tools add elements, connect them, copy, paste, delete, arrange and undo; the properties panel changes what's selected. Each change is made on the model and the text is written again from it. Elements can be dragged; their positions travel in the JSON. Everything happens in the browser: the app keeps the text.

**The consumer provides** the text (`value` / `onChange` or `defaultValue`; default: a small class diagram) and optionally the format (`format` / `onFormatChange` or `defaultFormat`, `mermaid`, `plantuml` or `json`; default `mermaid`), `onDiagramChange(result)` (each reading: the diagram, or null, and its problems), `templates` (the "New diagram" menu, as Mermaid or PlantUML text, each written in the current format when it can hold that kind; default: one per diagram type), `height` (560px, both panes on wide screens), `fileName` (`diagram`, for Download), `autoFocus` (false) and `className`.

- **Drawing tools** (a Toolbar: one Tab stop, arrow keys between tools): **Add** lists what this kind of diagram holds (class, interface, abstract class, enumeration, note; state, choice, fork, join; entity; participant, actor; C4 people, systems, containers, databases, queues, components, boundaries; actor, use case, system; action, decision, merge, start, end, flow end, fork, join, partition; node, device, execution environment, artifact, component, database, cloud), and the first four are also buttons on wide screens, which can be dragged onto the drawing to drop the element where it lands. A new element goes next to the selected one (in its namespace, composite state or boundary, or inside the frame when a frame is selected) and its name field takes focus. **Connect** opens a form (kind, from, to, label) filled from the first two selected elements; dragging the dot under an element onto another one does the same with the kind used last. **Delete** (or the Delete key on the drawing) removes the selection with the lines that touch it; what a removed frame held moves up a level. **Undo** and **Redo** (also Ctrl/⌘+Z, Ctrl/⌘+Shift+Z on the drawing) step through the tools' changes, dragged positions included; typing starts a new history, since the text has its own undo. **Copy**, **Paste** and **Duplicate** (Ctrl/⌘+C, X, V, D on the drawing) copy the selected elements with what they hold and the lines between them; pasted ones get fresh names and land a little down and right. **Layout** lays everything out again and turns the flow (top to bottom, left to right, bottom to top, right to left; the directions the format can keep). F2 or a double-click renames an element on the drawing; Select area and the align bar come from the UML Diagram.
- **Properties**: the name (renaming a class, state, entity or participant renames its id everywhere it's used when the name can be an id, otherwise sets its label), stereotype and members of a class (one field per member, typed as in Mermaid, `+name: String` or `+total()$`; each row's menu sets visibility, static, abstract, moves or removes it; Add attribute and Add method add a row and focus it), an entity's attributes, a state's description, a C4 element's description, technology and whether it's external; a relation's kind, label, multiplicities or ER ends, technology, and Reverse. Text fields apply when they lose focus or on Enter; choices apply at once.
- The tools write the whole text again: comments and the order of lines in hand-written Mermaid aren't kept. While the text has problems, the tools are off (writing it again would drop what couldn't be read) and say so.
- **Sequence diagrams**: Add holds participant, actor, note and the blocks (loop, alt, opt, par, critical, break: a block wraps the selected step); Connect adds a message after the selected step. The side panel lists the participants and steps as buttons (Ctrl, ⌘ or Shift to pick several, the keyboard path to what the drawing's clicks select), and the selected step's form changes a message's ends, text, arrow and activations, a note's text, place and participants, an activation, or a block's kind, condition and sections (Add section, Remove section for alt, par and critical); Move up and Move down move a step within its list (the diagram's, or a block section's); at the end of the list, focus goes to the other button. Participants say whether they are an actor. Deleting a participant removes the steps that use it.
- Switching format converts what could be read (Mermaid, PlantUML, JSON); text that couldn't be read stays as it is. A format that can't hold the current kind is disabled in the switch: Mermaid for use case and deployment diagrams, PlantUML for activities (they're read from PlantUML, but written back in Mermaid or JSON). Pasting text in another syntax (`@startuml`, a Mermaid header, `{`) switches the format to it. Mermaid and PlantUML have no positions, so they're kept in JSON only; Layout offers the directions the format can keep (PlantUML: top to bottom and left to right).
- Copy gives the current diagram in the current format, with dragged positions in JSON. Download gives it as text (`.mmd`, `.puml` or `.json`) or as an SVG or PNG image of the drawing (through UmlDiagram's `toImage`; the menu says if the image couldn't be made).
- The status line ("Class diagram, 7 elements", "3 problems", "1 item deleted · Class diagram, 6 elements") is the one polite status message; problems are also marks in the code with their message.
- On narrow screens the text, the drawing and the properties stack, and Copy and Download keep only their icon.
- For your own tools, the same changes are plain functions in `@/lib/uml-edit` (`addElement`, `addRelation`, `deleteItems`), `@/lib/uml-edit-fields` (`setElementFields`, `setRelationFields`, `renameElement`, `reverseRelation`, `memberFromText`), `@/lib/uml-edit-sequence` (`insertMessage`, `insertNote`, `insertBlock`, `setStep`, `moveStep`, `addSection`, `removeSection`), `@/lib/uml-edit-clipboard` (`copyItems`, `pasteItems`, `duplicateItems`) and `@/lib/uml-arrange` (`alignBoxes`, `setDirection`, `clearLayout`).

**In an app:** `pnpm dlx shadcn@latest add @coreloom/diagram-editor`.
