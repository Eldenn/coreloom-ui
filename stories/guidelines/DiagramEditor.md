# Diagram Editor

Software diagrams as text: Mermaid or Coreloom's UML JSON on the left, the drawing on the right with its own tools. Problems are underlined on their line and listed under the text, and what can be read still draws. The drawing tools add elements, connect them, delete and undo; the properties panel changes what's selected. Each change is made on the model and the text is written again from it. Elements can be dragged; their positions travel in the JSON. Everything happens in the browser: the app keeps the text.

**The consumer provides** the text (`value` / `onChange` or `defaultValue`; default: a small class diagram) and optionally the format (`format` / `onFormatChange` or `defaultFormat`, `mermaid` or `json`; default `mermaid`), `onDiagramChange(result)` (each reading: the diagram, or null, and its problems), `templates` (the "New diagram" menu, as Mermaid; default: one per diagram type), `height` (560px, both panes on wide screens), `fileName` (`diagram`, for Download), `autoFocus` (false) and `className`.

- **Drawing tools** (a Toolbar: one Tab stop, arrow keys between tools): **Add** lists what this kind of diagram holds (class, interface, abstract class, enumeration, note; state, choice, fork, join; entity; participant, actor; C4 people, systems, containers, databases, queues, components, boundaries), and the first three are also buttons on wide screens. A new element goes next to the selected one (in its namespace, composite state or boundary, or inside the frame when a frame is selected) and its name field takes focus. **Connect** opens a form (kind, from, to, label) filled from the first two selected elements; dragging the dot under an element onto another one does the same with the kind used last. **Delete** (or the Delete key on the drawing) removes the selection with the lines that touch it; what a removed frame held moves up a level. **Undo** and **Redo** (also Ctrl/⌘+Z, Ctrl/⌘+Shift+Z on the drawing) step through the tools' changes; typing starts a new history, since the text has its own undo.
- **Properties**: the name (renaming a class, state, entity or participant renames its id everywhere it's used when the name can be an id, otherwise sets its label), stereotype and members (one per line, as in Mermaid) of a class, an entity's attributes, a state's description, a C4 element's description, technology and whether it's external; a relation's kind, label, multiplicities or ER ends, technology, and Reverse. Text fields apply when they lose focus or on Enter; choices apply at once.
- The tools write the whole text again: comments and the order of lines in hand-written Mermaid aren't kept. While the text has problems, the tools are off (writing it again would drop what couldn't be read) and say so.
- Sequence diagrams get Add (participant, actor) and Connect (call, reply, asynchronous message); their steps are edited in the text.
- Switching format converts what could be read (Mermaid ↔ JSON); text that couldn't be read stays as it is. Mermaid has no positions, so they're kept in JSON only.
- Copy and Download give the current diagram in the current format (`.mmd` or `.json`), with dragged positions in JSON.
- The status line ("Class diagram, 7 elements", "3 problems", "1 item deleted · Class diagram, 6 elements") is the one polite status message; problems are also marks in the code with their message.
- On narrow screens the text, the drawing and the properties stack, and Copy and Download keep only their icon.
- For your own tools, the same changes are plain functions in `@/lib/uml-edit` (`addElement`, `addRelation`, `deleteItems`) and `@/lib/uml-edit-fields` (`setElementFields`, `setRelationFields`, `renameElement`, `reverseRelation`).

**In an app:** `pnpm dlx shadcn@latest add @coreloom/diagram-editor`.
