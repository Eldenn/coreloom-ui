# Diagram Workspace

> **Experimental.** This item may still change in a minor release, with an **Upgrading** note in the changelog. See [VERSIONING.md](https://github.com/Eldenn/coreloom-ui/blob/main/VERSIONING.md).

Where a document of several diagrams is written: its name, whether it's saved, the list of its views, and the diagram editor for the view that's open. The app keeps the document and saves it; every change comes back through a callback.

**The consumer provides** `title` and `onTitleChange` (the name, edited in place with EditableText), `diagrams` (`{ id, title, value, format, annotations? }[]`: each view's text in `mermaid`, `plantuml` or `json`, and statuses on its elements, see UML Diagram), `activeId` and `onActiveChange`, `onDiagramChange(id, value, result, format)` (the open view's new text, what could be read from it, and its format), `onAdd(kind)` (a diagram kind from "Add a diagram"; add the view and usually open it), `onImport({ title, value, result, format })` (optional: a file picked with "Import a file…" at the end of the Add menu, or dropped on the workspace, read in the browser; `title` is its name without the extension; add the view and usually open it; several dropped files call it once each, in order; without it the menu offers no import and dropped files are left alone), `onRename(id, title)`, `onReorder(ids)`, `onDelete(id)`, `saveState` (`saved`, `saving`, `unsaved` or `error`), and optionally `savedAt`, `onRetrySave` (shown with SaveStatus), `actions` (the app's buttons, such as Share, at the end of the top bar), `onAnnotationSelect(element, viewId)`, `height` (the whole workspace's height on wide screens, the editor taking what's left under the top bar; default: none, the editor's panes are 560px) and `className`.

- The list of views is a navigation landmark. The open view is marked as current. Each view has Rename (a field in place: Enter or leaving saves, Escape cancels) and Delete.
- Delete asks first, in a Popconfirm. The last view can't be deleted: its button is off and says why. After a delete, focus goes to the open view in the list.
- "Import a file…" takes the same files as the editor's Import (Mermaid, PlantUML, JSON or text, 1 MB at most) and gives the format from the extension or the first words. A file that can't be read doesn't call `onImport`; a polite status under the list says why.
- With `onImport`, files dragged over the workspace show a dashed "Drop diagram files…" hint over it, and each dropped file becomes a view, even when dropped on the editor (its text is not replaced: the editor's own Import does that). Dropping is for the pointer only; the Add menu's "Import a file…" is the keyboard and screen reader path.
- Reorder switches the list to a SortableList: drag by the handle or use the up and down buttons, then Done.
- The editor is DiagramEditor, controlled: the text, the format switch, the drawing tools, Copy and Download all work, and each change calls `onDiagramChange`. Switching views opens that view's text with its own undo history.
- To give the editor the whole window, put the workspace in `<AppShell fullWidth>` and set `height="calc(100svh - 7.5rem)"` (the window minus AppShell's header and gutters). A long list of views scrolls on its own. Below 1024px `height` is ignored: the panes stack at their own heights and the page scrolls.
- The list of views sits beside the editor when the workspace is at least 56rem wide (its own width, not the screen's: with a side panel open next to it, the list moves into the "Views" sheet).
- The open view's `annotations` (the findings of a review against the code, say) put a ring and a badge on their elements in the editor's drawing; the text stays editable. With `onAnnotationSelect`, a badge is a button that hands back the element and its view: show its findings beside the workspace. Annotations follow element ids, so the app updates or drops them when the text changes. Downloads leave them out.
- On a phone the list moves into a side sheet opened by "Views (3)"; picking a view closes it.
- The save state is announced politely when it settles, not each time it passes through Saving…

**In an app:** `pnpm dlx shadcn@latest add @coreloom/diagram-workspace`.
